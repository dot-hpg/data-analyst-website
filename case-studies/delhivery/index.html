Turning 144,867 raw pipeline rows into a clean, model-ready table of 14,817 delivery trips, and showing that real trips take about 2.1 times as long as the routing engine plans.

The brief

What the business case asked

Delhivery is a large Indian logistics company. Its pipelines log every package movement as many rows: one trip is split into segments, and each segment into checkpoints with running-total time and distance columns. In this form, the data can't be used for forecasting.

The task was to clean the data, rebuild each trip, engineer features for ETA forecasting, test whether planned times and distances match reality, and turn the results into a model-ready dataset and business recommendations.

Key findings

What the data showed

2.1x

Trips take twice the plan

The median trip takes 2.1 times the time planned by the OSRM routing engine, and 99% of trips run slower than plan.

26 vs 70

Speed is overestimated

Real median speed is about 26 km/h, while OSRM assumes about 70 km/h.

~39%

Hubs eat the time

About 39% of scan-to-scan time isn't spent driving. That's about 1.8 hours per trip.

44%

Three states lead

Maharashtra (18%), Karnataka (15%) and Haryana (11%) are the top origin states.

707

The busiest corridors

Bhiwandi ↔ Mumbai has 707 trips and Delhi ↔ Gurgaon has 513.

~674

Trips per day

The data covers 22 days, from 12 Sep to 3 Oct 2018.

Hypothesis tests

Do the plans match reality?

Paired tests at α = 0.05, with Wilcoxon signed-rank as the deciding test because the differences aren't normal. With this much data every test rejects H0, so the practical gap is what matters.

Recommendations

What the business should do

1

Show customers realistic ETAs

About 2x the routing estimate overall and 2.5-3x inside Mumbai and Delhi NCR, updated weekly per lane.

2

Cut hub waiting time first

Add docks and staff at Gurgaon Bilaspur, Bhiwandi and Bengaluru Nelamangala during the 8 pm to midnight peak.

3

Run scheduled off-peak shuttles

On Bhiwandi ↔ Mumbai and Delhi ↔ Gurgaon/Noida.

4

Grow capacity where demand is

Start with Maharashtra, Karnataka and Haryana, which make up 44% of trips.

5

Review the weakest operations

Send operations reviews to Muzaffarpur, Kolkata and Mumbai, and copy what works in Bengaluru.

Method

How I did the analysis

Two-stage merge

Rows become 26,368 segments, then 14,817 trips, with the right aggregation (first, last or sum) chosen for every column.

A built-in check

Confirms that the last running total equals the segment maximum in 100% of segments.

Feature engineering

City, place, code and state from facility names, plus calendar features, trip duration, corridor, delay ratio, speed and hub handling time.

Outliers capped, not dropped

About 14% of trips are IQR outliers, nearly all genuine long-haul FTL trips. Capping keeps them and cuts skewness from about 3.5 to 1.2.

Encoding

One-hot for route type, data, time of day, day of week and top 10 states. Frequency encoding for high-cardinality centres, cities and corridors.

Scaling

StandardScaler (saved) and MinMaxScaler (compared), producing 14,817 rows × 67 features.
