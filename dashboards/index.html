Power BIOperations

Supplier Performance & SLA Optimisation

Tracks how well each supplier meets its service-level agreement across store sizes and regions, so the operations team knows which contracts to renegotiate. Consumables hit 99.3% SLA compliance for the year, while uniforms lagged at 89.5% and dipped further in August.

Questions it answers

  • ›Which suppliers miss their SLA, and in which months?
  • ›How do store takings and headcount vary by region and store size?
  • ›Where should supplier reviews focus first?

Power BIAudit & compliance

Audit Efficiency & Effectiveness

Shows how many audits fall due each month, how often they run remotely and how hours are spread across auditors and countries. A key-influencers visual explains what makes an audit more likely to be booked on time, and the data points planning towards the United Kingdom, which has the most operating hours and orders.

Questions it answers

  • ›How many audits are due, by year and by month?
  • ›Which auditors carry the most operating hours?
  • ›Which countries should audit planning prioritise?

Power BIEducation

Education Data Insights

Compares subject scores across academies and between disadvantaged and non-disadvantaged students, then ranks schools on the Basics 5+ measure. It challenges the assumption that the gap is the same everywhere, and surfaces Basics 5+ as a strong single metric for comparing schools.

Questions it answers

  • ›How do test scores differ by subject and school?
  • ›How large is the gap for disadvantaged students?
  • ›Which schools lead on the Basics 5+ measure?
