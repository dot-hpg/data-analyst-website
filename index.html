Featured project

Walmart — Black Friday Spending Analysis

Do women spend more than men on Black Friday? I applied the Central Limit Theorem and bootstrap confidence intervals to 550,068 transactions, at three sample sizes and three confidence levels, for gender, marital status and age. Men spend about 8% more per transaction, but product mix explains the whole gap: within the same category, women pay the same. I also built a Tableau dashboard for the customer insights.

From query to insight

Insight lab

Real queries and code from my case studies, next to the charts they produce. Pick a question, then hover the chart.

7_1_late_delivery_vs_review_score.sqlsql
1WITH d AS (
2  SELECT o.order_id,
3    TIMESTAMP_DIFF(o.order_delivered_customer_date,
4                   o.order_estimated_delivery_date, DAY) AS days_vs_estimate
5  FROM target.orders AS o
6  WHERE o.order_status = 'delivered'
7),
8r AS (
9  SELECT order_id, AVG(review_score) AS review_score
10  FROM target.order_reviews
11  GROUP BY order_id
12)
13SELECT
14  CASE
15    WHEN d.days_vs_estimate <= -15 THEN '15+ days early'
16    WHEN d.days_vs_estimate <= -8  THEN '8-14 days early'
17    WHEN d.days_vs_estimate <= 0   THEN '0-7 days early'
18    WHEN d.days_vs_estimate <= 3   THEN '1-3 days late'
19    WHEN d.days_vs_estimate <= 7   THEN '4-7 days late'
20    ELSE '8+ days late'
21  END AS delivery_bucket,
22  COUNT(*) AS orders,
23  ROUND(AVG(r.review_score), 2) AS avg_review_score,
24  ROUND(100 * AVG(CASE WHEN r.review_score <= 1
25                       THEN 1 ELSE 0 END), 2) AS pct_1_star
26FROM d JOIN r ON d.order_id = r.order_id
27GROUP BY delivery_bucket;
Target Brazil · 95,824 delivered orders with reviews

Do late deliveries hurt review scores?

Early or on timeLate
Insight

Arriving early barely moves the score, but arriving late drags it down fast: orders 8+ days late average 1.70 stars, and 70% of them get 1 star. Reliability matters more than speed.

How I work

My analysis process

Every project follows the same five steps, so the result is something a team can trust and act on.

  1. 1

    Ask

    Pin down the decision the analysis must support, and the metric that will show it worked.

  2. 2

    Prepare

    Profile, clean and join the data, and write down every assumption.

  3. 3

    Analyse

    Query, explore and test, checking that each result holds up statistically.

  4. 4

    Visualise

    Pick the chart that makes the point in seconds, then build the dashboard.

  5. 5

    Recommend

    Turn findings into ranked actions with the evidence and the expected impact.
